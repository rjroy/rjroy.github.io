SUMMARY
The meeting reviewed the completed "Intent-Driven Development" essay and produced a companion LinkedIn "Thought" post. The essay articulates how AI-integrated software engineering is shifting the fundamental unit of work from implementation to intent definition and verification. The conversation centered on adapting the essay for LinkedIn, where platform constraints require different editorial choices than long-form writing.
