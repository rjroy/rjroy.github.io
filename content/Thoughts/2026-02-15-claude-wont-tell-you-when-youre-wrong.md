title: "Claude Won't Tell You When You're Wrong"
date: 2026-02-15
tags:
  - ai
  - cognition
---

As good as Claude is, it won't always tell you when you're barking up the wrong tree. If you tell it to start exploring something, it will start exploring that thing. It also does a thing where it will try to find the shortest path that it thinks is the shortest path, not realizing that taking the long way around can, a lot of times, be shorter because of all the mistakes and fixes you have to make along the way otherwise.
