## Question

What is the relationship between iteration and specification in AI partnership?

## Answer

You cannot write rules comprehensive enough for every situation. Instead, build a rhythm where AI proposes, you react, AI adjusts, and you refine your mental model. Over time, rules become less about constraint and more about shared vocabulary.
