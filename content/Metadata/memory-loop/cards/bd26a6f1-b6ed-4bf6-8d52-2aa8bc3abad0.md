## Question

What three conditions cause composability to break down in an agent-native architecture?

## Answer

Operations bundle decision logic (e.g., 'analyze and categorize' instead of separate primitives); output is formatted for humans rather than machines; and the agent cannot discover operations because help output is incomplete or unstructured.
