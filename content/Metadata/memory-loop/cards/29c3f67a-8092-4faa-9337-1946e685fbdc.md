## Question

What percentage of systems marketed as 'AI agents' actually meet genuine agentic criteria according to Gartner?

## Answer

Only 4% of 3,000+ systems marketed as 'AI agents' actually meet genuine agentic criteria; the rest are rebranded automation or assistants.
