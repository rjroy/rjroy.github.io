## Question

What does the inverted U-curve of AI assistance show about learning outcomes?

## Answer

Automated AI (high assistance) produced the lowest learning outcomes despite highest perceived ease, intermediate AI (moderate assistance) achieved the highest learning outcomes, and manual (no AI) fell in the middle.
