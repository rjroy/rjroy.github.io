## Question

What concept did Gotthold Lessing introduce in his 1766 work 'Laocoon'?

## Answer

Lessing argued that painters must choose the 'pregnant moment'—the instant from which preceding and succeeding actions are most easily comprehensible. Depicting the moment just before invites viewers to complete the narrative themselves.
