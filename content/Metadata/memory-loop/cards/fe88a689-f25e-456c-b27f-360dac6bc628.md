## Question

What did Herzog and Bryce (2007) find about the relationship between openness and mystery?

## Answer

Completely open scenes paradoxically reduce mystery because nothing is hidden. The optimal stimulus requires curved pathways and partial visibility to maintain mystery and exploration motivation.
