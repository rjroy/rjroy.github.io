## Question

What did a 2025 study of 666 participants by Gerlich find about cognitive offloading and critical thinking?

## Answer

Cognitive offloading mediates the relationship between AI tool usage and critical thinking decline, with younger participants showing both higher AI dependence and lower critical thinking scores.
