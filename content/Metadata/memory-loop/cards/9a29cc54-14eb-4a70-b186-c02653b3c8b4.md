## Question

What makes the 'Ground' step genuinely novel compared to existing PKM frameworks?

## Answer

Existing frameworks assume you encounter information then capture it. The Ground step explicitly separates 'understand context before capturing,' creating space for AI-assisted research before personal synthesis.
