## Question

What are the three relevant subfactors of the HSP scale?

## Answer

Ease of excitation (being overwhelmed by stimuli), aesthetic sensitivity (deep appreciation for visual arts), and low sensory threshold (discomfort with bright lights and chaotic scenes).
