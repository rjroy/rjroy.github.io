## Question

What are the three main categories of PKM tools in the early 2026 landscape?

## Answer

Traditional tools (plain-text Obsidian, original GTD apps), LLM-enhanced tools (Obsidian with Smart Connections, Notion AI, Reflect), and AI-native tools (Mem.ai).
