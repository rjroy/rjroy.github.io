## Question

According to Marvin Zuckerman's construct, what design preferences do high sensation-seekers have compared to low sensation-seekers?

## Answer

High sensation-seekers prefer designs that are complex and asymmetrical; low sensation-seekers prefer designs that are simple and symmetrical.
