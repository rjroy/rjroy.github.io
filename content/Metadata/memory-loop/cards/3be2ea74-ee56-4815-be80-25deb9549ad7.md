## Question

What is the difference between confirmation bias in humans and architectural predispositions in AI?

## Answer

Confirmation bias in humans is a cognitive shortcut where people seek information confirming existing beliefs. Architectural predispositions in AI are training-based features that shape what sources feel authoritative, what framing feels natural, and what conclusions seem reasonable—not based on the model 'believing' something.
