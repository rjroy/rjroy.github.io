## Question

What does the Chamorro-Premuzic et al. (2009) study reveal about Openness to Experience and artistic preferences?

## Answer

Openness to Experience is the single most consistent predictor of visual preferences across personality-aesthetics research, with correlations around r=.21 for abstract art forms like cubism.
