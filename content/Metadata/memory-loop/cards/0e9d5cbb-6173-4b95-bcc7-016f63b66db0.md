## Question

What did Grinschgl, Papenmeier, and Meyerhoff's 2021 experiments show about external tool use?

## Answer

Participants who offloaded information to external tools showed faster task completion but significantly worse recall, suggesting a near-mechanical relationship between convenience and forgetting.
