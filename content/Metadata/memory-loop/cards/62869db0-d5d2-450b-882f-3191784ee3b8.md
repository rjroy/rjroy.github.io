## Question

What are the three structured interactive rituals in the 'Think' phase?

## Answer

1) /daily-debrief - AI asks questions, user answers and reflects; 2) /weekly-debrief - AI prompts synthesis, user provides it; 3) /expand-note - AI identifies gaps in quick captures, user fills them in
