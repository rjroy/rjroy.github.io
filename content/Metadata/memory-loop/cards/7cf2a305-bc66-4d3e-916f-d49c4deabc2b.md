## Question

What is the difference between field-independent and field-dependent individuals regarding visual environments?

## Answer

Field-independent individuals can analytically separate figures from backgrounds and tolerate busy backgrounds. Field-dependent individuals perceive fields holistically and need simpler, cleaner backgrounds with clear structure.
