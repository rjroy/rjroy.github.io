## Question

What are the three key mechanisms for effective human-AI collaboration identified in research?

## Answer

AI delegation (AI passing tasks to humans in relevant contexts), capability complementarity (leveraging non-overlapping strengths), and explicit human oversight of synthesis and judgment.
