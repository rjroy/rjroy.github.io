## Question

What is the Einstein Test for Notes in the context of agentic PKM?

## Answer

If an agent can't parse what you meant, maybe you haven't actually worked it out yet. The agent's confusion becomes a proxy signal for your own potential confusion—a thinking prompt rather than quality control.
