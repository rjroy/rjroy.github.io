## Question

How are cost optimization levers applied in the agentic PKM system?

## Answer

Batch processing (analyze daily notes at end of day), threshold gating (skip notes below N words), Haiku triage (use Haiku to decide worth deeper analysis before Sonnet), and separate processes (run cheap confusion checks more frequently than expensive contradiction checks).
