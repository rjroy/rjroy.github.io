## Question

How did developers at Microsoft conceptualize multi-agent AI systems?

## Answer

They conceptualized AI agents as 'teams' of specialists (e.g., assistants, reviewers) working together in a human-like collaboration model, where AI team members could take on dominant or assistive roles.
