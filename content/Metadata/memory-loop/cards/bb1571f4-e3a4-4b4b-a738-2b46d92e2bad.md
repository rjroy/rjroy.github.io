## Question

Why is a Claims Index necessary in addition to RAG for contradiction detection?

## Answer

Embeddings find similarity, not contradiction. Semantically similar phrases like "I love X" and "I hate X" appear close in embeddings. The claims index provides structured assertions that make contradiction detection reliable, with RAG providing full context for detailed analysis.
