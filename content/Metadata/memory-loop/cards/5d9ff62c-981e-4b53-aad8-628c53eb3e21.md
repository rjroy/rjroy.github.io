## Question

How do Compass Rose and Spiral Grove relate to each other?

## Answer

Compass Rose handles what to work on (backlog management and prioritization), while Spiral Grove handles how to build it (structured development methodology). Large items detected by /start-work can be handed off to Spiral Grove's /spec-writing command.
