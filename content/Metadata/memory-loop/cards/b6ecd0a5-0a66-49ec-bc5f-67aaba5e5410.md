## Question

What does the 'Teach, don't code' design principle mean in Adventure Engine of Corvran?

## Answer

RPG mechanics are delivered as documents the AI reads, not logic the application executes. The bet is that an AI with good reference material makes better game-mastering decisions than hard-coded rules.
