## Question

What four actions does the article recommend for using AI research effectively?

## Answer

1) Ask multiple models the same question and note divergence, 2) Shape context deliberately to get different perspectives, 3) Cross-check with primary sources when decisions matter, 4) Understand your own predisposition in choosing which AI tool to use.
