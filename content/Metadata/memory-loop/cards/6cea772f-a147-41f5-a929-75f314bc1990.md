## Question

What are the six options available in the highlight + right-click menu in Pair Writing Mode?

## Answer

Validate (fact-check claims), Critique (analyze clarity, voice, structure), Tighten (make more concise), Embellish (add detail and nuance), Correct (fix typos and grammar only), and Polish (correct and improve prose with small, controlled improvements).
