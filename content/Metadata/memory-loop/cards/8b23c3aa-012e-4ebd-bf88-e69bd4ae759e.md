## Question

What is the recommended trigger timing for confusion checks versus contradiction/connection checks?

## Answer

Confusion checks could run real-time since they're cheap and provide valuable immediate feedback. Contradiction and connection checks should batch at end of day for cost efficiency and to avoid interruptions.
