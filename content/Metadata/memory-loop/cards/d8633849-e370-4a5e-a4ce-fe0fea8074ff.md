## Question

What is the continuum in human-AI interaction frameworks as described in the research?

## Answer

The continuum ranges from 'AI as instrument' (pure tool) to 'AI as peer collaborator,' with phases including Adaptive Instrument, Advisor, Co-pilot, and full partner roles that can shift with context.
