## Question

What proportion of matching to non-matching information maximizes aesthetic appreciation according to Jakesch and Leder (2009)?

## Answer

Moderate ambiguity maximizes both liking and perceived interestingness. Neither complete coherence nor complete incoherence optimizes aesthetic response—the proportion of matching to non-matching information is the key predictor.
