## Question

How does the Intent-Driven Development framework distinguish intent engineering from prompt engineering?

## Answer

Prompt engineering is described as surface-level syntax, while intent engineering is the ability to understand a problem deeply enough to describe what you want, why you want it, and how to know when you have it.
