## Question

What three human-in-the-loop patterns work in production for agent systems?

## Answer

Gate-based approval (explicit sign-off before sensitive actions), confidence-based escalation (human review when AI certainty falls below threshold), and edit-and-approve flows (human modifies AI output before execution).
