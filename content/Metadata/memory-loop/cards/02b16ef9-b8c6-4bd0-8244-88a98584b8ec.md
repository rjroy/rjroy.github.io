## Question

What three conditions are required for composability to work in an agent-native architecture?

## Answer

The daemon's operations must be genuinely atomic (one action per endpoint, no bundled workflows); output must be structured for machines (JSON) rather than humans (prose, tables); and the agent must have parity — every operation available to a human must be available to the agent.
