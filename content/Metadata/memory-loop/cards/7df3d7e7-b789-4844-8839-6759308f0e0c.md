## Question

What does the inverted U-curve of AI assistance in note-taking indicate?

## Answer

Intermediate AI assistance achieved the highest learning outcomes, while automated AI (high assistance) produced the lowest outcomes despite highest perceived ease of use, and manual note-taking fell in the middle.
