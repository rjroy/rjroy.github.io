## Question

Why did the PC reboot during model switching in the testing scenario?

## Answer

Multiple factors: model overlap (58GB + 20GB = 78GB in VRAM simultaneously) creating power draw spike of 300-400W, combined with a faulty KVM with ground fault that couldn't sustain the power delivery
