## Question

What is the Einstein Test for Notes in agentic PKM design?

## Answer

If an agent can't parse what you meant, it may indicate you haven't actually worked it out yet. The agent's confusion becomes a proxy signal for your own potential confusion—used as a thinking prompt rather than quality control.
