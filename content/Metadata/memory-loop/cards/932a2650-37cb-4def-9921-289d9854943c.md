## Question

What is the 'cathedral effect' in cognitive science?

## Answer

A phenomenon where spatial openness (high ceilings, open valleys, freedom to move) primes relational thinking, causing people in open spaces to generate more abstract connections and see more possibilities. This occurs unconsciously.
