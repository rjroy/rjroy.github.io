## Question

What does research show about the relationship between AI assistance and learning outcomes?

## Answer

An inverted U-curve exists: high AI assistance (automation) produces the lowest learning outcomes, intermediate AI assistance produces the highest learning outcomes, and no AI assistance falls in the middle.
