## Question

How did Leung et al. (2012) extend the cathedral effect research?

## Answer

Leung et al. (2012) had participants sit inside versus outside a cardboard box, finding that those outside the box generated significantly more correct answers on the Remote Associates Test of creativity. The effect replicated with free walking versus constrained paths and in virtual environments with Second Life avatars.
