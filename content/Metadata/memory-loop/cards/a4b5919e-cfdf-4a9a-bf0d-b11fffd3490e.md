## Question

What are the four phases of the LLM-Integrated PKM workflow?

## Answer

Ground → Capture → Think → Recall. Ground provides summaries and context, Capture is low-friction intake of ideas, Think is open-ended synthesis with structured rituals, and Recall uses lightweight editing and search interfaces.
