## Question

What did Herzog and Bryce (2007) find about very open scenes in relation to mystery?

## Answer

Herzog and Bryce (2007) found that very open scenes paradoxically reduce mystery because nothing is hidden. Mystery's effect depends on visual access, with optimal stimuli offering curved pathways and partial visibility.
