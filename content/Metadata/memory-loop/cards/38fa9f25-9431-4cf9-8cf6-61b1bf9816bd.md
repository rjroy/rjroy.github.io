## Question

What does 'trust calibration goes both ways' mean in AI partnership?

## Answer

The human decides how much to trust AI output, but the AI also functionally decides how much latitude it has. The rules written and how the human responds to pushback shape whether the AI asks or guesses when given vague instructions, and whether it states disagreements or complies.
