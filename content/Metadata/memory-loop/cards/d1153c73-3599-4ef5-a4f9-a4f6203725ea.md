## Question

What is the primary difference between the hierarchical (Mayor) model and the blackboard model?

## Answer

In the hierarchical model, a coordinator assigns work with top-down control and a single orchestration point. In the blackboard model, agents self-select work with emergent coordination and distributed decisions.
