## Question

What did Jakesch and Leder (2009) conclude about ambiguity in artworks?

## Answer

Jakesch and Leder (2009) found that moderate ambiguity in artworks maximizes both liking and perceived interestingness. Neither complete coherence nor complete incoherence optimizes aesthetic response—only the proportion of matching to non-matching information predicted appreciation.
