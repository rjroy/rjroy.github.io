## Question

What are RFC 2119 keywords used for in community best practices?

## Answer

RFC 2119 keywords (MUST, SHOULD, MAY) are used for clarity in rules: MUST indicates absolute requirement enforceable in CI, SHOULD indicates strongly recommended, and MAY indicates optional.
