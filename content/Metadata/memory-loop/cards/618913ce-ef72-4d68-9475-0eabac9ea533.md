## Question

What is the core idea behind Progressive Discovery in Lore Development?

## Answer

You discover the problem in layers through multiple modes, cycling until understanding emerges. Research existing solutions, discover a pattern, realize you need to brainstorm how it applies. Brainstorm wild options, hit a constraint, research whether others solved it. Specify a rough draft, spot gaps, cycle back.
