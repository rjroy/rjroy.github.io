## Question

What is the main difference between a single-destination background and a crossroads background in terms of viewer engagement?

## Answer

A single-destination background positions the viewer as a passive observer looking at a complete artistic vision, while a crossroads background positions the viewer as an active protagonist invited to make a choice between different paths.
