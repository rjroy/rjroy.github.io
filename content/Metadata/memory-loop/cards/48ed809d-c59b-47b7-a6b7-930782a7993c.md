## Question

According to the Kaplan framework, what is 'mystery' in environmental psychology?

## Answer

Visual features suggesting more to be discovered. It is a key predictor of landscape preference and exploration motivation, and works best with curved pathways and partial visibility that promise revelation without providing it.
