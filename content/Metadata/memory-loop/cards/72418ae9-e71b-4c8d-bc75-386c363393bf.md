## Question

What is Gotthold Lessing's concept of the 'pregnant moment'?

## Answer

The pregnant moment is the instant from which preceding and succeeding actions are most easily comprehensible. Depicting the moment just before the climax invites viewers to complete the narrative themselves through imagination.
