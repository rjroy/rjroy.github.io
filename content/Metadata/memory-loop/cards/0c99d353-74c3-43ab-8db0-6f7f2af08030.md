## Question

What did Vartanian et al. (2015) discover using fMRI about spatial environments?

## Answer

Open architectural spaces activate dorsal stream structures involved in visuospatial exploration, while enclosed spaces activate regions associated with avoidance motivation. Open rooms trigger approach decisions.
