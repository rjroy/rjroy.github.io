## Question

What are architectural predispositions in AI models?

## Answer

Architectural predispositions are training-based features that shape what sources get weighted heavily, what framing feels natural, what conclusions seem reasonable, and where skepticism gets applied. They are baked into a model's training and are not the same as confirmation bias.
