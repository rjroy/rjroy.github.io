## Question

What did the 2023 Amaro et al. study find about HSP individuals' visual advertising preferences?

## Answer

High-SPS individuals demonstrate more positive attitudes toward advertisements with less visual stimuli; they prefer static over dynamic, minimal over complex, and chromatic over achromatic visuals.
