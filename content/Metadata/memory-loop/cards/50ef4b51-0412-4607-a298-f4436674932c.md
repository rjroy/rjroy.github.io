## Question

What is the relationship between architectural predispositions and effective AI partnership?

## Answer

Effective AI partnership requires deliberately building awareness of each model's predispositions by asking the same questions to multiple models, noting divergence, testing assumptions, and treating synthesis as perspective rather than fact.
