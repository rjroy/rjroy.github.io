## Question

What was Lessing's 'pregnant moment' concept in his 1766 work Laocoon?

## Answer

Lessing argued that painters should choose the 'pregnant moment'—the instant from which preceding and succeeding actions are most easily comprehensible. Depicting the moment just before the climax invites viewers to complete the narrative themselves, engaging their imagination.
