## Question

What is the key difference between human confirmation bias and AI architectural predispositions?

## Answer

Human confirmation bias is a cognitive shortcut where people seek information confirming existing beliefs. AI architectural predispositions are built into the model's training—they shape which sources get weighted heavily, what framings feel natural, and what conclusions seem reasonable.
