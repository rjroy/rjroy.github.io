## Question

What is 'need for cognitive closure' and how does it affect response to ambiguity?

## Answer

Need for cognitive closure (NFC) is a personality trait where some individuals strongly prefer definite, unambiguous answers and experience anxiety from open-ended stimuli. High-NFC individuals tend to 'seize and freeze' on information quickly and prefer predictability.
