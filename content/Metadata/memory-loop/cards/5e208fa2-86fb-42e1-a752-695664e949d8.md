## Question

What makes the 'Ground' step genuinely novel in this PKM approach?

## Answer

Existing frameworks assume information is encountered then captured. Ground separates 'understand context before capturing,' creating space for AI-assisted research before personal synthesis, recognizing that modern knowledge work often requires understanding a landscape before contributing to it.
