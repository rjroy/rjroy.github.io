## Question

What are the four key stages of the Ground → Capture → Think → Recall PKM pattern?

## Answer

1) Ground - Research and understand context before acting, 2) Capture - Quick intake without friction, 3) Think - Process, synthesize, and connect through conversations, 4) Recall - Find and use knowledge when needed
