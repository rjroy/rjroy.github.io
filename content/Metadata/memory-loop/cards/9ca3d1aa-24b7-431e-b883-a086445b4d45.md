## Question

What is the purpose of the 'What Changed?' flow in Pair Writing Mode?

## Answer

It allows the human to highlight their rewritten section and have Claude analyze what changed between the original Claude version and the human's rewrite, creating a learning loop where both parties improve by understanding revision patterns.
