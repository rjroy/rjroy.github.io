## Question

What is the continuum described in human-AI interaction frameworks?

## Answer

The continuum ranges from 'AI as instrument' (pure tool) to 'AI as peer collaborator,' with phases including Adaptive Instrument and role-based modes like Advisor, Co-pilot, and full partner.
