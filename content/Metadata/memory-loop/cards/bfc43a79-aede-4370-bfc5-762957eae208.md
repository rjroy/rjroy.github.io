## Question

What is 'need for cognitive closure' and how does it relate to ambiguous stimuli?

## Answer

A personality trait where people prefer definite, unambiguous answers and experience anxiety from open-ended stimuli. Those high in need for closure negatively appreciate ambiguous artworks and would find open-ended imagery distressing.
