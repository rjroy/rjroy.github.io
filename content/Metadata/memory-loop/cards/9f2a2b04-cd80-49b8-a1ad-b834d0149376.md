## Question

Why did the Gilbert and Sullivan partnership fracture after 25 years despite complementary skills?

## Answer

Sullivan wanted to be taken seriously as a composer but felt subservient to Gilbert's witty libretti, causing purpose divergence rather than skill redundancy
