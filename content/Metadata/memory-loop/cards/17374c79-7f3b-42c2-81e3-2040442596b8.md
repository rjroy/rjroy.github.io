## Question

What is sufficient to determine if expressiveness in tools is working effectively?

## Answer

Personal preference alone is not enough; patterns in how humans respond to spatial openness, visual ambiguity, and choice architecture must be understood to distinguish sustainable expressiveness from novelty.
