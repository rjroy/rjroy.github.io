## Question

What did Iyengar and Lepper's 2000 jam study demonstrate about choice?

## Answer

Iyengar and Lepper found that 24 jam options produced only 3% purchases versus 30% with 6 options, demonstrating choice overload and paralysis. A meta-analysis by Chernev et al. (2015) confirmed that excessive choice increases decision deferral, switching, and regret.
