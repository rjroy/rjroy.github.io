## Question

What is the six-step emergent capability feedback loop in an agent-native system?

## Answer

(1) Build atomic primitives with full parity, (2) users ask agents for unanticipated outcomes, (3) agents compose primitives to achieve them or fail, (4) observe patterns of what succeeds, fails, and is asked for repeatedly, (5) add primitives (not features) to fill gaps, (6) repeat.
