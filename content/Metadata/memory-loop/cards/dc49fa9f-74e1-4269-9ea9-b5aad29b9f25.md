## Question

What neural structures are activated by open versus enclosed architectural spaces according to Vartanian et al. (2015)?

## Answer

Open architectural spaces activated dorsal stream structures involved in visuospatial exploration. Enclosed spaces activated the anterior midcingulate cortex, a region with amygdala projections associated with avoidance motivation.
