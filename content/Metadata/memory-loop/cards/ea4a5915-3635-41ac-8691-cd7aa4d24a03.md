## Question

What is Guild Hall, in terms of its purpose and organizing metaphor?

## Answer

Guild Hall is a multi-agent workspace for delegating work to AI specialists and reviewing what they produce. Its organizing metaphor is a craftspersons' guild, where workers have identities, souls, and specializations that persist across tasks.
