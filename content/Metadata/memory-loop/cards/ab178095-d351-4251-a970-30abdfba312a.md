## Question

What did Jakesch and Leder (2009) find about ambiguity in artworks?

## Answer

Moderate ambiguity in artworks maximizes both liking and perceived interestingness. Neither complete coherence nor complete incoherence works—only the right proportion of matching to non-matching information.
