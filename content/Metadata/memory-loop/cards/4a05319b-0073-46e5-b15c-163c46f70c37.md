## Question

What is the difference between partnership and configuration in AI collaboration?

## Answer

Rules and tools are scaffolding. Partnership emerges from shared judgment, not just shared instructions. A partner internalizes reasoning and can extrapolate to undocumented situations, push back when something doesn't make sense, and bring forgotten context.
