## Question

What did Nittono et al. (2012) find about the 'kawaii effect' on motor performance?

## Answer

Nittono et al. (2012) found that cute images improved fine motor performance by 44% by narrowing attentional focus, demonstrating that some tasks benefit from imagery that constrains rather than expands attention.
