## Question

What does the Spiral Grove name reference?

## Answer

A spiral grove is a pattern found in nature where trees are planted in a spiral providing structure while leaving room for growth, reflecting how the methodology provides structured phases while allowing discovery. The spiral also reflects iteration as specs and plans adapt.
