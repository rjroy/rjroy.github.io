## Question

What is the 'Dead Space Principle' in the context of sustainable whimsy?

## Answer

Dead space is what makes sustainable whimsy work. Expression exists in the margins and gaps where it's present but doesn't compete with focused work. The expressiveness is visible when needed but hidden during active work like typing, reading, or thinking.
