## Question

What are the three distinct categories in the PKM tool ecosystem as of early 2026?

## Answer

Traditional (plain-text Obsidian, original GTD apps), LLM-enhanced middle tier (Obsidian Smart Connections, Notion AI, Reflect), and AI-native extreme (Mem.ai).
