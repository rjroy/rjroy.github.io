## Question

What did Gotthold Lessing argue about in 'Laocoon' (1766)?

## Answer

Painters must choose the 'pregnant moment'—the instant before climax—rather than depicting the climax itself, because depicting the moment before invites viewers to complete the narrative with their imagination.
