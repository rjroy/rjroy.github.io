## Question

What preference-performance gap did Microsoft Research find in a 405-student study comparing note-taking and LLM use?

## Answer

Note-taking alone or combined with LLMs had significant positive effects on retention compared to LLM alone, yet students preferred using LLMs and perceived them as more helpful despite worse outcomes.
