## Question

Why is context described as the currency of partnership in AI collaboration?

## Answer

A contractor needs detailed specs because they lack domain knowledge. A partner who has absorbed context can respond to shorthand instructions like 'make it consistent with how we handle errors elsewhere' without needing exhaustive documentation.
