## Question

What was the core issue with Spec-Driven Development (SDD) that led to the creation of Lore Development?

## Answer

SDD appeared to be documentation for AI but was actually documentation for humans. The AI treated specs as complete with no gaps, and when gaps existed, the AI invented answers rather than filling them from previous findings and research.
