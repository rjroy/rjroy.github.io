## Question

Why is AI research not neutral?

## Answer

When asking AI to research a question, you receive sources weighted as authoritative by that model's training, synthesis through that model's architectural lens, and conclusions shaped by that model's training. This filters the research through the AI's predispositions rather than providing objective synthesis.
