## Question

What is the metaphorical origin of the name 'Guild Hall'?

## Answer

A place where skilled workers gather, check a board for available work, and coordinate without a central authority dictating assignments. The guild metaphor implies autonomous agents with expertise who self-select tasks rather than receiving top-down orders.
