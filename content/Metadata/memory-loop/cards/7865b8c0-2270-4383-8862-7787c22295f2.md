## Question

What are the three core entities in the Guild Hall design?

## Answer

Projects (define task source and worker capacity limits), Workers (ephemeral agents that pick up tasks, record progress, perform work, and report completion), and Recruiter (monitors the blackboard and spawns workers when capacity is available).
