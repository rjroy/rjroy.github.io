## Question

What three main problems does Memory Loop's Pair Writing Mode solve?

## Answer

It closes the loop between manual edits and AI edits (coexisting in split view), reclaims authorship without losing assistance (human edits while Claude advises), and creates a learning loop where human and AI improve through the 'what changed?' flow.
