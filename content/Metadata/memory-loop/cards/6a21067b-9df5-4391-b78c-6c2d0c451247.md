## Question

What mechanism explains why threshold imagery maximizes appreciation?

## Answer

The mechanism is imaginative completion: threshold imagery activates inference processes, extending viewing time and recruiting memory and planning systems. Moderate ambiguity activates more fixations and stronger right fronto-parietal activity as the brain works toward resolution.
