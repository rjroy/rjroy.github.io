## Question

What are the four stages of the Ground → Capture → Think → Recall pattern?

## Answer

1) Ground - Research and understand context before acting; 2) Capture - Quick intake without friction; 3) Think - Process, synthesize, and connect; 4) Recall - Find and use knowledge when needed
