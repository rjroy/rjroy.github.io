## Question

What is the 'cathedral effect' and what does it demonstrate about spatial openness?

## Answer

The cathedral effect, demonstrated by Meyers-Levy and Zhu (2007), shows that participants in rooms with high ceilings form fewer, more abstract categories and engage in relational processing, while those under low ceilings focus on concrete details. High ceilings activate freedom-related concepts unconsciously, promoting relational thinking and seeing connections.
