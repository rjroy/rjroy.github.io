## Question

What is the distinction between whimsy-as-decoration and whimsy-as-meaning?

## Answer

Whimsy-as-decoration is surface ornamentation, while whimsy-as-meaning is playfulness that reinforces product values. Only the latter produces lasting user loyalty.
