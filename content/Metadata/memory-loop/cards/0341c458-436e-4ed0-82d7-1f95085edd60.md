## Question

What is the correlation between AI tool usage and critical thinking abilities according to 2025 research?

## Answer

A strong negative correlation of -0.68 was found between AI tool usage and critical thinking abilities, with younger users (17-25) showing higher AI dependence and lower critical thinking scores.
