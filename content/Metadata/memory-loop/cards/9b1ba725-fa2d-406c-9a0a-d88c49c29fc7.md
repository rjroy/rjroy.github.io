## Question

What does the Kaplan framework identify as optimal for landscape preference and exploration?

## Answer

The Kaplan framework identifies 'mystery'—visual features suggesting more to be discovered—as a key predictor of landscape preference and exploration motivation. Curved pathways and partial visibility are optimal, promising revelation without providing it.
