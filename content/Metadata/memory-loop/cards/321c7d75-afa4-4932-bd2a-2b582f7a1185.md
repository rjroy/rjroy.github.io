## Question

What is the core tension identified in the research about LLMs in personal knowledge management?

## Answer

What feels productive (frictionless capture, conversational synthesis) can systematically prevent learning and retention. The challenge is distinguishing desirable difficulties that create learning from unnecessary friction.
