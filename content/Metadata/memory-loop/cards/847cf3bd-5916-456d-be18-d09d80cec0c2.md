## Question

What does Shelf Judge explicitly state it is not, regarding BoardGameGeek?

## Answer

Shelf Judge is not a BGG replacement; game discovery, reviews, forums, and marketplace belong to BGG, and Shelf Judge pulls metadata from BGG and stays in its lane.
