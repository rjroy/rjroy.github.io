## Question

What are the five workflow sections that encode partnership philosophy?

## Answer

Research before acting, Plan before executing, Review with fresh context, Ask first boundaries (for new files, architectural changes, dependencies), and Never behaviors (no skipping tests, no unread code changes, no declaring victory without verification).
