## Question

What did Vartanian et al. (2015) find using fMRI regarding open versus enclosed spaces?

## Answer

Vartanian et al. (2015) found that open architectural spaces activated dorsal stream structures involved in visuospatial exploration, while enclosed spaces activated the anterior midcingulate cortex, a region with amygdala projections associated with avoidance motivation. Open rooms triggered approach decisions; enclosed ones triggered exit decisions.
