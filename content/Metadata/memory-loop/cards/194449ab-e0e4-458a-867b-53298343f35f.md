## Question

What is the main difference between a single destination background image and a crossroads background image in terms of viewer engagement?

## Answer

A single destination background positions the viewer as a passive observer looking at a complete artistic vision, while a crossroads background positions the viewer as an active protagonist invited to make a choice about which path to take.
