## Question

What visual preferences do high-openness individuals show compared to low-openness individuals?

## Answer

High-openness individuals prefer all art forms, show greater tolerance for visual complexity and novelty, and appreciate variety over familiarity. Low-openness individuals prefer plain, straightforward, familiar imagery.
