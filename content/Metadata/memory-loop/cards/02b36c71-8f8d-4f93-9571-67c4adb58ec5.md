## Question

What are the three tiers of the PKM landscape?

## Answer

Traditional PKM (manual organization, keyword search, human synthesis), LLM-Enhanced PKM (same structures with semantic search and AI summaries), and AI-Native PKM (designed around conversational interfaces and emergent organization).
