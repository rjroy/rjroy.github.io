## Question

According to Microsoft Research, which learning method produced the best retention outcomes in a study of 405 students?

## Answer

Note-taking alone or combined with LLMs had significant positive effects on retention compared to LLM use alone, despite students preferring and perceiving LLMs as more helpful.
