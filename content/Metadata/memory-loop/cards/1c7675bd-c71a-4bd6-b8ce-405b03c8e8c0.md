## Question

What six foundational patterns emerge from cross-domain partnership research?

## Answer

Trust as the essential substrate, productive tension between explicit contracting and implicit understanding, complementarity balanced with shared purpose, effective asymmetry management, predictable evolutionary phases, and consistent failure modes.
