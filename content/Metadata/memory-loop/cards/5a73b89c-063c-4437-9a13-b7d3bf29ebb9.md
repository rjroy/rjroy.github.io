## Question

How do field-dependent and field-independent individuals differ in their tolerance for busy backgrounds?

## Answer

Field-independent individuals can work effectively with complex, detailed backgrounds because they naturally filter irrelevant visual information. Field-dependent individuals need simpler, cleaner backgrounds with clear structure because background elements compete for their attention.
