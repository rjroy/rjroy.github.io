## Question

What did Herzog and Bryce (2007) find about the relationship between visual openness and mystery?

## Answer

Very open scenes paradoxically reduce mystery because nothing is hidden. The optimal stimulus offers curved pathways and partial visibility, promising revelation without providing it.
