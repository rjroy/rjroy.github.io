## Question

How does AI differ from traditional tools like compilers, linters, or search engines in terms of relationship model?

## Answer

Traditional tools have a transactional relationship (input, output, done), while AI is a reasoning partner that synthesizes information and makes judgment calls based on patterns learned during training.
