## Question

What did Mueller and Oppenheimer's studies find about note-taking methods?

## Answer

Students taking longhand notes outperformed laptop note-takers on conceptual understanding despite writing fewer words, because verbatim transcription bypasses the selective, generative processing that creates learning.
