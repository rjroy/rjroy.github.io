## Question

How does Intent-Driven Development differ from Spec-Driven Development in terms of what drives development?

## Answer

In Intent-Driven Development, the real driver is layered intent refined through conversation, research, brainstorming, and iteration, while Spec-Driven Development frames the specification as the input that drives development.
