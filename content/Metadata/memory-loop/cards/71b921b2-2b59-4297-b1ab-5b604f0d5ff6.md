## Question

What does the article mean by 'the tool shapes what you see'?

## Answer

Different AI tools with different architectural lenses make different questions easily answerable. For example, Claude's skeptical lens makes 'What could go wrong?' easy to answer, while ChatGPT's collaborative lens makes 'What's possible?' easy to answer.
