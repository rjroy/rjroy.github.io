## Question

What is the role of AI in the 'Think' phase according to the structured rituals?

## Answer

AI acts as a Socratic questioner rather than as a synthesis generator. It asks questions in /daily-debrief and /weekly-debrief, and identifies gaps in /expand-note, but the user produces the synthesis through active retrieval and articulation.
