## Question

What are the four phases of the Ground → Capture → Think → Recall PKM workflow?

## Answer

1) Ground - Provides summaries and contextual insight before diving in; 2) Capture - Low-friction intake of ideas and notes into an inbox; 3) Think - Open-ended conversational synthesis with structured rituals; 4) Recall - Lightweight editing and search interfaces for information accessibility
