## Question

What is 'need for cognitive closure' and how does it relate to ambiguous imagery?

## Answer

Need for cognitive closure is a personality trait where people prefer definite, unambiguous answers and experience anxiety from open-ended stimuli. Ganczarek et al. (2021) found that need for closure negatively predicted appreciation of ambiguous artworks.
