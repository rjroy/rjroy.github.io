## Question

What are the three key moderators that determine whether openness helps or hurts cognition?

## Answer

The three key moderators are: (1) Task demands—creative tasks benefit from open imagery while focused analytical tasks benefit from simpler backgrounds; (2) Need for cognitive closure—high-NFC individuals experience anxiety from ambiguity; and (3) Expertise—low-expertise individuals experience more choice overload with complex options.
