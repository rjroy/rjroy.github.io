## Question

What is Eysenck's arousal theory explanation for why introverts and extraverts prefer different visual stimulation levels?

## Answer

Introverts have higher baseline cortical arousal and reach optimal stimulation faster, requiring less environmental input. Extraverts have lower baseline arousal and require external stimulation to reach comfortable levels.
