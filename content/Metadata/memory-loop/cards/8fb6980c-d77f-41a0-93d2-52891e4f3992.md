## Question

What are the five levels of the AI autonomy spectrum?

## Answer

Level 0: Tools (stateless, single-shot, user-invoked); Level 1: Assistants (conversational context, user-initiated); Level 2: Automation (predefined workflows on triggers/schedules); Levels 3-4: Semi-autonomous agents (multi-step planning, method choices); Level 5: True autonomous agents (long-horizon goals, proactive monitoring, minimal oversight).
