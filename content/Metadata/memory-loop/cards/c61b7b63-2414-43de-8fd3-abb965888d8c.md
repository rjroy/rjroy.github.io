## Question

What is AGENTS.md and what standard does it represent?

## Answer

AGENTS.md is an emerging cross-tool standard for AI coding instructions, stewarded by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation. It has adoption in 60,000+ repositories and supports 25+ AI tools.
