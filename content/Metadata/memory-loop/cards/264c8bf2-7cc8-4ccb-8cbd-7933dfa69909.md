## Question

What distinction does the article make between sustainable expressiveness and novelty?

## Answer

Sustainable expressiveness can be understood through patterns in how humans respond to spatial openness, visual ambiguity, and choice architecture, whereas novelty that wears thin relies only on personal preference.
