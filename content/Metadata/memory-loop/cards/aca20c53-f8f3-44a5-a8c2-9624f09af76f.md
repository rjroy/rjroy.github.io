## Question

How do users describe the reciprocal interaction pattern in AI partnership?

## Answer

Users describe it as a back-and-forth loop where the AI's output is treated as a draft or suggestion, then responded to with corrections or improvements, creating 'push-and-pull' creative friction similar to brainstorming with a colleague.
