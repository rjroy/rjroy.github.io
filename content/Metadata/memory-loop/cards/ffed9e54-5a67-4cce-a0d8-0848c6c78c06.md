## Question

According to the document, what approach beats specification in AI partnerships?

## Answer

Iteration beats specification. You cannot write rules comprehensive enough to cover every situation, but you can build a rhythm: AI proposes, you react, AI adjusts, and rules become less about constraint and more about shared vocabulary.
