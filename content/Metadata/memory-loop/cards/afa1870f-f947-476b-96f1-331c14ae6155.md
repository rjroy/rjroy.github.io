## Question

What did Leung et al. (2012) find about spatial confinement and creativity?

## Answer

Participants sitting outside a cardboard box generated significantly more correct answers on the Remote Associates Test of creativity compared to those inside the box. The effect replicated with free walking versus constrained paths and in virtual environments.
