## Question

Why is Spiral Grove called 'Spiral Grove'?

## Answer

A spiral grove is a natural pattern of trees planted in a spiral that provides structure while leaving room for growth, reflecting how the methodology structures phases while allowing discovery. The spiral also reflects iteration as specs and plans adapt.
