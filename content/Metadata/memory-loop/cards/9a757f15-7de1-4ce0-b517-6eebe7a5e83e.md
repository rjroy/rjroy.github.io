## Question

What did Vartanian et al. (2015) discover using fMRI about spatial environments?

## Answer

Open architectural spaces activate dorsal stream structures involved in visuospatial exploration, while enclosed spaces activate regions associated with avoidance motivation. Open spaces trigger approach decisions as the brain treats spatial openness as an affordance for exploration.
