## Question

What are the three main problems that Memory Loop's Pair Writing Mode solves?

## Answer

It closes the loop between manual edits and AI edits by showing them in split view, reclaims authorship without losing AI assistance, and creates a learning loop where both human and AI improve through the 'what changed?' flow.
