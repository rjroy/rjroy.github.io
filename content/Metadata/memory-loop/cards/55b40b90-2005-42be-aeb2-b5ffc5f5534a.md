## Question

What is Sönke Ahrens's view on the role of Zettelkasten in the AI era?

## Answer

He views the Zettelkasten as a dialogue partner that becomes more critical in the AI era when thinking is understood as happening both externally and internally, rather than seeing AI as a replacement of thinking.
