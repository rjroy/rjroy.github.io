## Question

What are the three options for Claims Index maintenance?

## Answer

Auto-extracted (complete coverage but higher cost and extraction errors), Synthesis-triggered (aligns with workflow but new claims not indexed until next synthesis), or Hybrid (light extraction on capture with full reconciliation on synthesis).
