## Question

What did the 2025 DORA report find about the relationship between AI-driven individual productivity gains and organizational delivery metrics?

## Answer

AI boosted individual output (more tasks completed, more PRs merged) while organizational delivery metrics stayed flat, and friction and burnout persisted unchanged.
