## Question

What does the inverted U-curve reveal about AI assistance and learning outcomes?

## Answer

High AI assistance (automation) produces the lowest learning outcomes despite highest perceived ease; intermediate AI assistance produces the highest learning outcomes; no AI assistance (manual) falls in the middle.
