## Question

What is the 'cathedral effect' as demonstrated by Meyers-Levy and Zhu (2007)?

## Answer

Participants in rooms with high ceilings formed fewer, more abstract categories and showed relational processing, while those under low ceilings focused on concrete details and item-specific features. High ceilings activated freedom-related concepts unconsciously.
