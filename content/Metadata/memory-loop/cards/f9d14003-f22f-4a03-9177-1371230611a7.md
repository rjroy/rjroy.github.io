## Question

What are the three options for claims index maintenance?

## Answer

Auto-extracted (complete coverage but extraction errors compound), Synthesis-triggered (aligns with workflow but new claims not indexed until next synthesis), and Hybrid (light extraction on capture, full reconciliation on synthesis).
